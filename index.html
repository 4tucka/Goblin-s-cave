Everything a DM needs, down to the last goblin

🗺️ Map Builder

Square or hex grids, paintable terrain, fog of war, token placement and object scaling — over uploaded maps or hand-drawn templates.

👹 NPC & Monster Spawner

Build stat blocks with HP, AC and token icons, or import them as JSON. The DM drives every monster on the initiative bar.

🎲 Dice & Chat

A full dice tray (d4–d100) with /roll 1d20+5 commands, advantage/disadvantage, and a table chat for RP and OOC talk.

🔊 Atmosphere Engine

Synthesized ambient loops — Dungeon Rain, Tavern Noise, Cave Drips — plus your own MP3s or audio URLs, synced to the room by the DM.

⚔️ Live Sessions

Real-time token movement, fog reveals, turn tracking and ready-up lobbies. Open a second tab and watch the table sync.

👺 The Goblin Guide

A floating companion who explains the site, answers D&D 5e rules questions, and conjures NPCs, encounters and loot on demand.

Your session in five steps

1.Forge the room — name it, set a password and player cap if you like.
2.Build the map — pick a template or upload your own; paint terrain and hide secrets in fog.
3.Spawn the threats — add NPCs and monsters with HP, AC and tokens.
4.Set the mood — attach an ambient track for the table.
5.Post the room — share the code, wait for the party to Ready up, then Start Game Session.
